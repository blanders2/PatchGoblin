Hosts used by your projects and aliases from ~/.ssh/config. Nothing is saved until you press Save in the Server Manager.

Servers are saved in the Server Manager (the servers button at the bottom of the left bar) and shared with the rest of PatchGoblin. The wake-up packet is sent from this computer, so the server must be on the same LAN, or its wake-up address must be the subnet's broadcast address.
